THE PHILOSOPHY

Consistency before improvement.

You don't need to change everything every week. First, learn how to fit training and meal preparation into your actual life.

Then, look at the data. Identify patterns. Make one small adjustment and see what happens.

Build the habit. Understand the patterns. Then make deliberate adjustments.
